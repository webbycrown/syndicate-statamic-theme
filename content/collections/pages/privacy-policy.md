## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use this website.

### Information we collect

- Contact details you submit through forms (name, email, phone, message)
- Newsletter subscription emails
- Basic usage data such as pages visited and browser type

### How we use information

- To respond to enquiries and contact-form submissions
- To send newsletters only if you subscribed
- To improve site content and performance

### Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the site, or when required by law.

### Cookies

The site may use cookies for essential functionality and, if you accept them, analytics. You can change cookie preferences in your browser.

### Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through the Contact Us page.

### Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
